Key concepts
Naming a projection
  • Views are named by where the detector (image intensifier) sits. The X-ray tube is directly opposite, under the table.
  • RAO / LAO: the C-arm rotates around the patient's long axis toward their right or left shoulder.
  • Cranial / caudal: the detector tilts toward the head or the feet.
  • Image convention: the patient's left is on the right of the screen. The orange edge letters (L R A P H F) show which body direction each image edge faces.
Orientating an unknown image
  • Spine: projects on the image left in RAO and on the image right in LAO.
  • Catheter: in LAO the catheter and spine are on the same side; in RAO the catheter curves away across the heart.
  • Diaphragm: rises into the lower field in cranial views; falls away in caudal views.
  • LAD vs LCx: the LAD runs to the apex and gives septals; the LCx hugs the AV groove and gives OMs to the lateral wall.
The two cardiac planes
  • AV plane (teal, turn on Planes): the AV groove carrying the RCA and LCx. In LAO it is seen nearly en face, so the RCA and LCx make a ring; in RAO it is edge-on, so they run as a line.
  • Interventricular (IV) plane (violet): the septum carrying the LAD and PDA. In RAO it faces you; in LAO it is on profile, splitting left-sided from right-sided branches.
  • The Current view panel tells you how each plane is seen at the angle you are on.
Foreshortening and overlap
  • A segment pointing toward the beam looks short (end-on) and can hide a stenosis. Switch the colour to Foreshortening to see this on the 3D vessels: red is end-on, blue-white is lying flat in the image.
  • Each segment is clearest in views perpendicular to it. Tap a segment to see the band of good angles on the pad. The best view also avoids overlap with other branches.
  • Always image a lesion in at least two near-orthogonal views.
Classic view → structure
  • Distal LM / LAD–LCx bifurcation → LAO caudal ("spider"), AP caudal
  • LCx and OMs → RAO caudal
  • Mid LAD and diagonals → AP cranial, LAO cranial, RAO cranial
  • Mid–distal LAD / LIMA anastomosis → left lateral
  • Proximal–mid RCA ("C") → LAO; PDA length ("L") → RAO; crux → AP or LAO cranial
